### &nbsp; <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/hvRJCLFzcasrR4ia7z/giphy.gif" width="22" /> &nbsp; About Me

```yaml
name:        Akash Sakala
role:        AI Research Intern @ Voibe
degree:      B.E. AIML · B.M.S. College of Engineering · CGPA 9.75/10
focus:       LLM fine-tuning · knowledge distillation · quantization · ASR
building:    smaller, faster models that run in production & offline
```

I'm an AI/ML engineer who turns large research models into lean, deployable ones. At **Voibe** I fine-tune speech and language models — Whisper ASR with LoRA (4.51% WER on Indian-English), and a **120B → 20B → 4B** distillation pipeline that keeps **99.4%** of teacher accuracy at **~30× smaller** size. Previously an **SDET at Netradyne**, where I automated IoT flashing and internal tooling to cut hours of manual work down to minutes.

- 🔭 &nbsp; Shipping fine-tuned & distilled models on **[Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/Akash-Sakala)** — Whisper (Indic ASR), FLAN-T5, and a 4-bit distilled phishing classifier
- 🌱 &nbsp; Going deeper on **RLVR alignment, cross-tokenizer distillation & efficient inference**
- 💬 &nbsp; Ask me about **PyTorch, LoRA/PEFT, LangGraph agents, or squeezing big models onto small hardware**
